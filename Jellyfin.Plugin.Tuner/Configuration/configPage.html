Radio

Station names look like "Genre · Station". Empty hides live radio.

Jamendo

Leave empty to hide the Jamendo stations.
Kept tracks are saved here. Use a folder inside a Music library.

TV

Only the server uses it; users' browsers never get it.
For the Antenna channels, for example http://192.168.1.50. Leave empty to hide them.
Channel numbers separated by commas, for example 59.1, 39.2.
Number=name pairs separated by semicolons, for example 12.1=NBC (WTLV).

Home page

Each row waits while someone is pointing at it or scrolling it. Works in the browser, desktop and phone apps, not the TV apps.
Changes show up after a page reload.

My Media

Name=start..end pairs separated by semicolons, for example Halloween=10-01..11-01; Christmas=12-01..12-31. The name is a collection's name. In season it gets its own tile at the front of My Media and its playlists are shared with everyone; the rest of the year the tile is gone and its playlists are yours only.
Each library's tile gets a new random backdrop this often. 0 turns it off; use 0 if you upload your own library pictures, or they'll be replaced.

Get apps

A page of the apps below, under the avatar menu next to Profile and Display. Each person sees the apps for the device they're on first, and the rest under Other devices. Works in the browser, desktop and phone apps, not the TV apps.

Apps

Shown in this order. For a GitHub project (https://github.com/owner/repo), people get the newest release's file for their device, with its version. Tick "Always use this exact link" to hand out one specific download instead.

Watch together

People in the same SyncPlay group get a chat beside the video. Full screen hides it; elsewhere in Jellyfin it shrinks to a bubble in the bottom right. Messages stay in the server's memory only and are gone when the group ends. Each person needs SyncPlay allowed in their user settings (Dashboard, Users, the person, SyncPlay access). Works in the browser, desktop and phone apps, not the TV apps.

Music page

Search with genre tiles, a Catalog of playlists, decades and A to Z, Now Playing, and Radio, as tabs. Radio moves out of the top bar into it. Jellyfin's own music page is one click away under Classic view. Works in the browser, desktop and phone apps, not the TV apps.
Anything shorter is treated as a single and left off the album shelves. It still shows up in Search and on the artist's page.

TV section

TV opens Surf, with tabs for Guide (Jellyfin's Live TV page) and Classics (the library below). Live TV and that library leave the top bar and side menu, and in My Media the Live TV tile becomes TV. Their pages stay Jellyfin's own, with the tabs added on top.
The TV library's name exactly as it shows in Jellyfin, for example Classic TV & Cartoons. Empty: no Classics tab.

Movies section

Movies gets two tabs, Movies and Classics (the library below). That library leaves the top bar, side menu and My Media. Both stay Jellyfin's own pages, with the tabs added on top.
The movie library's name exactly as it shows in Jellyfin, for example Classic Movies.

Requests

Your Seerr or Jellyseerr address, for example https://requests.example.com. Set: a Request link joins the top bar and side menu and opens it in a new browser tab (the desktop app opens it in your browser). Empty: no link.
